Circular restricted three-body problem

The landscape of balance points

Two massive bodies circle each other; a third is too light to pull back. In the frame that turns with them, gravity and the centrifugal effect combine into the effective potential shown here. Its five flat spots are the Lagrange points. Click to drop a particle at rest, or drag to launch it. Hatched areas are closed to the selected particle by its Jacobi constant.

Periodic solutions

Islands of order

Almost every three-body start leads to chaos, yet a thin set of starting conditions returns exactly to itself. Each card computes one full period from the published initial conditions and loops it. Most of these orbits are unstable: run one in the simulator and watch it hold for a few laps before it breaks.

1687 – today

Three bodies, no formula

Newton gave the world an exact answer for two gravitating bodies. Add a third and the exact answer disappears. Over three centuries the search for it produced celestial mechanics, the first glimpse of chaos, and a hunt for rare periodic orbits that is still going.
